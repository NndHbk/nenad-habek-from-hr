KATICA Mail Hub

KATICA Mail Hub is a private personal application used by its owner to retrieve, organize and analyze authorized Gmail data for personal information-management and automation workflows.

Access to Gmail is authorized by the Google account owner through Google OAuth. The application requests read-only Gmail access and does not use that permission to send, modify or delete Gmail messages.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Purpose

The application helps the authorized user keep local information systems current and retrieve relevant information from their own mailbox.

Availability

KATICA Mail Hub is currently a private personal-use application and is not offered as a public commercial service.
